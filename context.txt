### THE NOVEL FINDING (robust, cross-dataset) — computed on test NODES
Fake news drifts LESS than real news (2-dim drift = cosine + L2, root masked):
- GossipCop: real 7.18 vs fake 6.39, ratio 0.890, t=-66.4, p<1e-16 (144,726 vs 75,263 nodes)
- Politifact: real 6.05 vs fake 5.35, ratio 0.885, t=-17.7, p=6e-70 (13,113 vs 18,091 nodes)
- Effect emerges at hop>=2; root drift zero by construction (not ~1.40 — that was old 5-dim def).
